🔗 Why SoulTix?

Explanation for judges and visitors about the on-chain anti-scalping mechanism.

🚫
Non-Transferable by Design
tickets is Soulbounded which cannot be transferred after minting. The transfer() function is overridden in the smart contract to always revert — no secondary market possible.
🎯
Anti-Bot & Anti-Scalping
A 2-ticket wallet limit combined with on-chain identity verification prevents bots from buying up tickets. Scalpers can't resell a ticket that cannot be transferred.
💸
Safe Refund Until D-2
Buyers can get a full refund up to 2 days before the event. The ticket is burned, funds are returned, and the slot reopens for another buyer — all on-chain.
🔍
Transparent & Verifiable
Every transaction is recorded on BOT Chain and can be verified by anyone on BOTScan. No centralized database that can be manipulated.
⚠️ Sybil Resistance Limitations :
Currently, 1 wallet = 1 buyer (may cover multiple attendees, e.g. a family). This system does not prevent someone from creating multiple wallets to buy more tickets (multi-wallet Sybil attack). A complete solution would require true Proof of Personhood integration (e.g. World ID, biometric KYC), which is future work for this project.

Identity Verification is Currently a Simulation (Dummy): The verifyMe() function in the contract allows anyone to mark themselves as verified without any real data check. This is intentional to demonstrate the flow of Proof of Personhood without building a full KYC integration within the hackathon timeframe. In production, only a trusted oracle/backend would call the verification function after processing identity data off-chain.
